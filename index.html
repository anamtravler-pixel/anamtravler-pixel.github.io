How a project goes

A simple path from first idea to final files.

1

Brief

We talk through the goal, the audience and the feel you want.

2

Concept

Sketches, references and a colour direction before anything is built.

3

Build

Modelling, illustration or layout, with check-ins along the way.

4

Motion & polish

Animation, lighting and fine detail until it feels finished.

5

Deliver

Final files in the formats you need, ready to publish.
